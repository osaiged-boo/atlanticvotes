JD Vance: 'The reason we lost is the war'

November 4, 2026

JD Vance posted on X on Wednesday morning with his own read of the 2026 midterm results, the morning after election night, as results continued to come in.

Vance argued that Republican turnout and the makeup of the electorate were not catastrophic compared with 2024. He pointed to Florida, which he said was 9 points more Republican than Democrat — but said many voters who backed Trump and Vance in 2024 voted for Democrats this time.

He said a pollster found the top reasons those voters gave for returning were ending the war, focusing on the cost of living and inflation, putting America First, and changing the relationship to Israel.

His conclusion: “the reason we lost is the war.”

Vance framed the loss as a message about the war and the cost of living, rather than about turnout. He warned against amnesty as a proposed solution, and urged supporters to channel their anger into electing Republicans who share their worldview.

This is Vance’s analysis, not Atlantic Votes’.
