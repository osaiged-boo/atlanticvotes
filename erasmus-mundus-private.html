How to apply

Programme list: the official EACEA Erasmus Mundus Catalogue (189 programmes, checked 26 November 2026) — eacea.ec.europa.eu/scholarships/erasmus-mundus-catalogue. There is no central application: you apply on each programme’s own website. The catalogue warns that some listed programmes may no longer offer scholarships, as their funding period may be ending — confirm scholarship availability on the programme’s official site. Partner universities and countries were harvested from each programme’s official site in November 2026 — 177 of the 189 programmes; the other 12 have no usable project link in the catalogue and stay “check official site”. Deadlines are shown only where verified on an official programme site; everywhere else, check the official site. Programmes flagged “No 2027 scholarship intake reported” are not presented as scholarship-available — confirm their status on the official site. Settlement summaries checked November 2026 — always confirm on the official immigration site of the country you plan to finish in. This is general information, not immigration advice.
