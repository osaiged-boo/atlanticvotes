Where the numbers come from

Preliminary results: Elections BC, results site — the only results source used on this page. Candidate list: Elections BC, final 2026 candidate list (nominations closed October 3, 2026). Election-night results are preliminary; the final count runs November 6–10, 2026 and Return Day is November 18, 2026. Status words (Leading / Elected) are Elections BC's own. "Projected" is this site's model: when the leader's margin is bigger than the votes that could still arrive, estimated from votes counted and polls reported, the riding is marked Projected — projections are not results and can change as more votes are counted. A riding Elections BC declares Elected is never overridden by a projection.
