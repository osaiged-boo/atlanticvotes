For Abiemwense — Medical Laboratory BSc, Nigeria, Second Class Lower (2.2), degree taught in English. These are not Erasmus Mundus courses, so there is no €1,400/month Erasmus scholarship here. These are UK courses, self-funded, with a UK settlement route instead.

The short answer

Leeds — Clinical Embryology and Assisted Reproduction Technology (full-time, on campus) is the best fit. It accepts a 2:2. Oxford, Dundee distance-learning, and Liverpool John Moores all ask for a 2:1, so a 2.2 does not meet their stated minimum. Dundee's on-campus Human Clinical Embryology course is also likely 2:1 — check it before spending the application fee.

UK settlement plan — on-campus courses only

1. Student visa — study in the UK for the 1-year MSc.
2. Graduate visa — after graduating, stay and work in almost any job, no sponsor or job offer needed. Under current rules, applications on or before 31 Dec 2026 get 2 years; applications from 1 Jan 2027 get 18 months (PhD graduates keep 3 years). She would graduate in 2028, so expect 18 months — check the rules again at the time.
3. Skilled Worker visa — switch before the Graduate visa ends if you have a qualifying job offer from a licensed sponsor.
4. Settlement — after 5 years on qualifying work routes, you can apply for indefinite leave to remain.

Distance-learning courses (Dundee IVF DL, Leeds DL) give no UK visa and no Graduate visa. Only on-campus study in the UK opens this route.

What to do first

1. Leeds full-time ART — check the official 2027 fee and apply early; this is the only clear 2:2 fit.
2. Book IELTS 6.5 (no less than 6.0 in any component) — every course on this page needs at least that, Oxford needs 7.5.
3. Keep the Erasmus Mundus route going in parallel — IMIM closes 4 Jan 2027 and is funded at €1,400/month, unlike these UK fees.
