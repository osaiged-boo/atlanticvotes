Takeaways

What the numbers say

  • The narrowest possible majority: the BC NDP won 47 of 93 seats — exactly the 47 needed — on 44.87% of the vote (944,579 votes).
  • The closest race was Surrey-Guildford, decided by 22 votes (Garry Begg 8,947 to Honveer Singh Randhawa 8,925); Kelowna Centre was decided by 40 votes, and four ridings were decided by fewer than 100 votes.
  • The Conservatives took 44 seats on 43.28% of the vote — 1.59 points behind the NDP, but 3 fewer seats.
  • The Greens held 2 seats on 8.24% of the vote; their leader, Sonia Furstenau, lost her own riding of Victoria-Beacon Hill to the NDP’s Grace Lore, while NDP leader David Eby won Vancouver-Point Grey and Conservative leader John Rustad won Nechako Lakes.
