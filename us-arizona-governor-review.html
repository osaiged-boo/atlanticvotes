Arizona governor review: Hobbs wins a second term

November 17, 2026

Democrat Katie Hobbs has won re-election as governor of Arizona, defeating Republican Andy Biggs. The Associated Press called the race for Hobbs early on Wednesday, November 4.

Arizona’s official count puts Hobbs at 1,434,824 votes and Biggs at 1,094,393, with about 99 per cent of the expected vote counted. That put Hobbs at about 56 per cent and Biggs at about 43 per cent.

Hobbs is the first Democrat re-elected as governor of Arizona since Janet Napolitano in 2006. She first won the office in 2022, narrowly defeating Republican Kari Lake.

The win capped a wider Democratic night in Arizona. Democrats also took control of the state House and the state Senate, giving the party its first trifecta in the state since 1966.

The results remain unofficial until the state canvass on November 23.
