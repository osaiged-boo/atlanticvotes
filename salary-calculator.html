This is a 2026 annualized estimate built from the CRA’s published federal and provincial tax brackets, basic personal amounts, CPP/CPP2 and EI rates, and Revenu Québec’s QPP rates. It assumes you claim the basic TD1 credits unless you choose otherwise in the optional section — claim only the personal amount, or no credits at all (claim code 0) — and spreads each annual maximum evenly across the year, so a real paycheque will differ once CPP and EI caps are reached mid-year, and with pay dates, bonuses, or taxable benefits.

Quebec figures use the QPP, the reduced Quebec EI rate, and the 16.5% federal tax abatement. Ontario tax includes the provincial surtax and health premium. RRSP contributions are treated as pre-tax; benefits and other deductions come off after tax. For exact payroll withholding, use the CRA’s Payroll Deductions Online Calculator or speak with an accounting professional.
