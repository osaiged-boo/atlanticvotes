Where the numbers come from

Unofficial results: Elections PEI — the only results source here. Candidates: Elections PEI, Officially Registered Candidates (Charlottetown, Summerside, Cornwall, Stratford, Three Rivers). Acclaimed means the candidate was elected by acclamation — a fact from the candidate list, not a projection. Elected is Elections PEI's own call and is never overridden. Projected is this site's model: the leading margin is bigger than the votes that could still arrive, estimated from votes counted and polls reported — projections are not results and can change. Too close to call means the margin is under 100 votes or under 0.5% of counted votes. Leading means ahead with no projection yet.
