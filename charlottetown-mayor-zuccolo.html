Amir Zuccolo elected Charlottetown mayor

November 4, 2026

Amir Zuccolo has been elected mayor of Charlottetown, winning the municipal election held on November 2, 2026. She led a seven-candidate race for the city’s top office, finishing well clear of the rest of the field.

Zuccolo received 5,125 votes. Philip Brown finished second with 3,580 votes, 1,545 votes behind Zuccolo. Mitchell G. Tweel was third with 2,054 votes, followed by Cecil Villard with 1,738. Daniel Mullen received 337 votes, Daniel Cousins received 102, and Anthony Fitzpatrick received 94.

In total, 13,030 votes were counted in the mayoral race, and all polls are reporting. Zuccolo’s 5,125 votes represent about 39 per cent of the votes counted in the race.

Brown received about 27 per cent of the votes counted, Tweel about 16 per cent, and Villard about 13 per cent. The remaining candidates each received less than 3 per cent of the votes counted.

According to CBC, Zuccolo is Charlottetown’s second female mayor and the first woman to hold the office in more than 50 years.

The results in this article are from Elections PEI only. Full results for Charlottetown, including the council ward races, are on Atlantic Votes’ PEI municipal results page, and the official results are published on Elections PEI’s results site.
