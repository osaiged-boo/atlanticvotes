Amir Zuccolo elected Charlottetown mayor

November 4, 2026

Amir Zuccolo has been elected mayor of Charlottetown, winning the municipal election on November 2, 2026 with 5,125 votes. She led a seven-candidate race and finished 1,545 votes ahead of her nearest rival, outgoing mayor Philip Brown, who received 3,580 votes.

Mitchell G. Tweel finished third with 2,054 votes, followed by Cecil Villard with 1,738. Daniel Mullen received 337 votes, Daniel Cousins 102 and Anthony Fitzpatrick 94. In total 13,030 votes were counted in the mayoral race and all polls are reporting. Zuccolo took about 39 per cent of the vote, while Brown took about 27 per cent, Tweel about 16 per cent and Villard about 13 per cent.

Zuccolo campaigned on a platform her campaign summed up as an affordable, sustainable and inclusive future for the city. On housing she promised a plan that builds, including turning vacant and derelict properties into homes. On transportation she called for a people-centred approach that better connects neighbourhoods. She also promised a more open city government, with fewer closed-door meetings and more civic engagement, and a city better prepared for extreme weather.

University Avenue was a recurring example in her campaign. She pointed to its vacant and derelict buildings as space that could be put to better use for housing, and she called for better bus service along the corridor, arguing that Charlottetown must keep developing a transit system that serves all neighbourhoods more often, including in the evenings and on weekends.

Her campaign describes her as a scientist, entrepreneur, educator and community leader. CBC reports that she is Charlottetown’s second female mayor and the first woman to hold the office in more than 50 years.

The results in this article are from Elections PEI only. Full Charlottetown results, including the council ward races, are on Atlantic Votes’ PEI municipal results page, and the official results are published on Elections PEI’s results site. Her full platform is on her campaign site.
