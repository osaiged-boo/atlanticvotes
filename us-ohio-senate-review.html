Ohio Senate review: Brown wins the seat back

November 4, 2026

Democrat Sherrod Brown has won Ohio’s special election for the United States Senate, defeating Republican Senator Jon Husted. The Associated Press called the race for Brown late on election night.

Its latest figures put Brown at 2,301,042 votes and Husted at 2,077,748, with about 98 per cent of the expected vote counted. That put Brown at about 50 per cent and Husted at about 45 per cent.

This was not a regular election for a full term. It was a special election to fill the remainder of the term JD Vance won in 2022 before he left the Senate to become vice president. Husted was appointed to that seat in January 2025. Brown will serve the final two years of the term, through January 2029, and would need to run again in 2028 for a full term.

For Brown it is a comeback. He previously represented Ohio in the Senate from 2007 to 2025, then lost his seat to Republican Bernie Moreno in 2024. The race was one of the most expensive in the country, with advertising spending reported at more than $500 million.
