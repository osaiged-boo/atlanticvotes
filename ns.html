Takeaways

What the numbers say

  • A clear PC majority: 43 of 55 seats on 52.82% of the vote (187,045 votes); 28 seats are needed for a majority.
  • The Liberals won more votes than the NDP — 80,849 to 79,068 — but far fewer seats: 2 to 9.
  • The closest race was Annapolis, decided by 8 votes (David Bowlby 3,289 to Carman Kerr 3,281).
  • The only Independent seat was Cumberland North, won by Elizabeth Smith-McCrossin with 3,567 votes.
