Takeaways

What the numbers say

  • A bare PC majority: 21 of 40 seats — exactly the 21 needed for a majority.
  • The Liberals were only 1,883 votes behind (86,668 to 88,551) but won 6 fewer seats (15 to 21).
  • Turnout was 52.3% of 381,818 electors.
  • The NDP and Independents hold 2 seats each, on 16,580 and 7,766 votes.
