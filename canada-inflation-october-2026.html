Canada's inflation rate flat year-over-year in October: StatsCan

November 16, 2026

Statistics Canada says consumer prices rose 3.1 per cent in October from a year earlier. The increase matched September's 3.1 per cent, leaving inflation flat on a year-over-year basis.

Energy did much of the work. Gasoline prices were up 30.9 per cent from October 2025, faster than September's 27.9 per cent. Statistics Canada said the jump was partly because crude oil prices were lower a year ago. Gasoline prices actually fell 2.5 per cent from September to October. Natural gas prices were also higher. Excluding energy, inflation edged down to 2.0 per cent from 2.1 per cent.

Groceries rose at a faster pace in October, ending four straight months of slower price growth. Fresh vegetables led the turn, up 1.4 per cent after falling 2.9 per cent in September. Sugar and confectionery and condiments, spices and vinegars also rose. Clothing prices were higher too. Cheaper cellphone plans and slower rent growth pulled the other way.

The result came in just below what economists expected. A Bloomberg survey of 12 economists had pointed to a 3.2 per cent rise on average.
