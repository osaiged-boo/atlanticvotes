Texas Senate review: Talarico flips the seat

November 4, 2026

Democrat James Talarico has won the Texas seat in the United States Senate, defeating Republican Ken Paxton. The Associated Press called the race for Talarico on election night.

Its latest figures put Talarico at 5,445,121 votes and Paxton at 4,249,752, with about 99 per cent of the expected vote counted. That put Talarico at about 55 per cent and Paxton at about 43 per cent.

Talarico is a 37-year-old four-term state legislator from Austin. Paxton is the Texas Attorney General. Paxton had beaten incumbent Senator John Cornyn in the Republican primary runoff, and Talarico will succeed Cornyn in January.

The win is historic in Texas. Talarico is the first Democrat Texas has sent to the Senate since Lloyd Bentsen in 1988, and the first Democrat to win any statewide race there since 1994. The contest was the most expensive Senate race of the midterm cycle, and its campaign turned on household costs and billionaire influence.
